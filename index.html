Safety, up front.

What to expect, what can go wrong, and who should not be treated. Melissa reviews your history at the consult.

FDA approved since 2002
Approved for cosmetic use since 2002, with a long, well-documented safety record. Administered by a board-certified nurse practitioner in a licensed medical office.
Common, temporary
Small red bumps, redness or minor bruising at the injection sites, usually resolving within a few hours to a few days.
Rare
Temporary eyelid or brow drooping, which usually resolves on its own within a few weeks.
Not for everyone
Not appropriate for people who are pregnant or breastfeeding, or who have certain neuromuscular disorders.
Not covered by insurance
Cosmetic treatment is not covered by insurance. Financing is available through Cherry if you would rather pay over time.
